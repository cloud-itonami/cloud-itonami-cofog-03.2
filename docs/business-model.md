# Business Model: Independent Fire-Risk Inspection Robotics

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-cofog-03.2`
- COFOG: `03.2` (Fire-protection services)
- Activity: fire-hazard survey and inspection reporting for buildings and
  vegetation-fire-exposed sites
- Social impact: fewer preventable fires, faster hazard remediation,
  auditable public-safety records

## Customer

- municipal fire departments contracting out routine inspection load
- large campuses / industrial sites required to self-certify fire safety
- insurers requiring an independent hazard survey
- rural/wildland-urban-interface property owners and municipalities

## Offer

- scheduled + on-demand fire-hazard survey (thermal, smoke-path, vegetation
  density)
- risk-scored inspection report with photo/sensor evidence
- remediation recommendation routed to the site owner
- immediate-hazard escalation straight to the fire department (human
  dispatch, never automated)
- inspection-history audit export for insurance/compliance

## Revenue

- per-site inspection fee
- municipal or campus subscription (recurring inspection contract)
- insurer-referral fee for independent verification
- compliance/audit export package

## Trust Controls

- risk-scoring recommendations require Fire-Risk Governor clearance
- any `:high`/`:safety-critical` finding always escalates to human dispatch
- every survey, finding and escalation is logged
- public hazard claims must reference measured sensor data
